  1. 01Software For AllBrand architecture and guidelines2026+

    Brand guidelines covering three brands: Software For All, Workplus and Software NI. The big decision was the architecture.

    Workplus and Software NI are the names people already know, so they lead. Software For All sits behind them with a lighter touch. We also took the programme sub-brands out of the guidelines so the system stayed simple to use.

  2. 02Chestnutt Holiday ParksTone of voice and social2026+

    A family-run business with holiday parks at Annalong, Loughside and Sandilands. Their brand guidelines had no tone of voice, so I wrote one.

    It's friendly and familiar, close to how the family actually talks, with guidance for each area of their work. I also built it into an AI skill, so drafts written with AI tools start out sounding like Chestnutt. Alongside that, I lead their social visuals.

    Through
    DHD
    My role
    Design lead, writer
    Scope
    Tone of voice, AI skill, social
