In short

I'm Daniel Young, Head of Design at DHD, a creative agency that does strategy, design, digital and film. I lead our brand and digital work and sit on the senior leadership team.

Most of my week goes on the questions before the design. Who is this brand for? What should it say, and what should it stop saying? Then I make it, with the team, down to the detail.

AI is part of that. I use it for research, first drafts, production and checks, and I build my own apps and tools with it. Building things taught me where it helps and where it gets in the way.

I also help brands get ready for it. More and more of a brand's words are now written with AI tools, by marketing teams, agencies and the client's own staff. If the brand isn't written down in a way those tools can follow, it drifts. So alongside guidelines, I write tone of voice skills and brand.md files that keep it on track.

Most of the work happens before anyone opens Figma.

01 How I use AI

Four rules I keep to.

  1. 01

    Machines draft, people decide.

    AI can give me twenty options. It can't tell me which one is right for the people the brand is for.

  2. 02

    Speed buys thinking time.

    The hours AI saves go back into the idea and the details, not into making more of the same.

  3. 03

    Say where it was used.

    I tell clients where AI helped and where it didn't, so nobody is guessing. This site does the same at the end of each note.

  4. 04

    A person checks everything.

    Every output is read, used and tested by me before it leaves my desk.
