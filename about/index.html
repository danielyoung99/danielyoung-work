About

Strategy, identity and digital, with a person in charge.

I'm Head of Design at DHD, a creative agency that does strategy, design, digital and film. I lead brand and digital work, and I work alongside the teams we make things for.

AI is part of my working day. I use it for research, first drafts, resizing, checks and code. I also build my own apps and tools with it, which is how I learned where it helps and where it gets in the way.

What stays with me: choosing the idea, writing the words that go on the page, type, spacing and colour, and deciding what ships. I check writing for machine habits before it goes out.

How I use AI

Four rules

01

Machines draft, people decide.

AI can give me twenty options. It can't tell me which one is right for the people the brand is for.

02

Speed buys thinking time.

The hours AI saves go back into the idea and the details, not into making more of the same.

03

Say where it was used.

I tell clients where AI helped and where it didn't, so nobody is guessing.

04

Check everything.

Every output is read, used and tested by a person before it leaves my desk.
