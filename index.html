03 How I work with AI

Machines draft. People decide.

AI is part of my working day. This is where it helps on a typical brand project, and where I take over.

StageThe machineMe

Research

Summarises meeting transcripts. Scans thirty competitors where I used to manage five.

Reads the parts the summary skipped. Talks to the people who run the business and the people who use it.

Options

A dozen headline routes in a minute. Most are fine. None are specific.

Uses them to find the obvious answer, then steers away from it.

Production

Resizes artwork, names files, exports logos and checks colour contrast.

Type, spacing, colour and the words on the page.

Sign-off

Checks drafts against the brand's own rules and flags anything that drifts.

Decides what ships. If I can't say why a choice is right, it isn't finished.
