# Overview

Colloide was seeking brand strategy, visual identity design, and website design to help reposition and futureproof their brand.

As workloads allowed, I was fortunately able to move from visual identity design into website design — likely one of the largest websites I have designed to date.

# Challenge

Sustainability needed to be the core focus of the new rebrand, but not in a way that felt heavy-handed or forced. The previous identity was never grounded in meaning and, as a result, lacked affinity with employees.

  02-logo-mark.jpg | Colloide logo mark.
  03-colour-palette.jpg | Colloide colour palette.

# Process

Extensive interviews with employees revealed a mystery surrounding the existing identity and strong support for a rebrand.

While the strategy came easily, with values being updated rather than defined from scratch, the visual identity required multiple iterations to narrow down to a concept.

  04-landscape.jpg | Colloide brand identity in use, image 1.

# Solution

The simple mark minimally depicts the Earth orbiting the Sun, linking into their vision for a brighter tomorrow.

Rounded corners, a vivid accent colour, and halo graphic devices reinforce the circular, sustainable narrative throughout the visual components.

# Results

Colloide’s internal team has managed the rollout of the brand. With no expense spared, they’ve redecorated offices and updated their fleet of vehicles – killing off their old visual identity quickly and effectively.
