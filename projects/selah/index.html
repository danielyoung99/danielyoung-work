Selah · Bible study for iPhone and iPad

Read the Bible, mark it up, and turn what you read into prayer.

Selah is a Bible study app for iPhone and iPad. Read in KJV, NKJV or NASB, highlight or underline verses in five colours, and write study notes with text, lists and photos. On iPad, notes can be handwritten with Apple Pencil. Any verse can become a prayer point, and prayer points can be shared through iCloud with up to eight people. Three reading plans, habit tracking and two widgets (Verse of the Day and Streaks) sit alongside.
