Stripes · Lawn care, logged

A lawn care log for every job, from mowing to scarifying.

Stripes is an iPhone app for keeping track of lawn care. The lawn on the main screen grows and fades as the next mow gets close, and a Mowed today button logs the job. Nine jobs from a lawn care calendar are built in, and custom jobs take their own name, icon, colour and months. Each entry can have a date, a note and a photo. The year view shows the calendar as a coloured grid, and Open-Meteo weather data suggests the best day to mow.
