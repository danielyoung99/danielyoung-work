  1. Designing for connection

    Designing for connection: Five key design components helping Stem Systems connect more than cables.

  2. 01 Strategy

    Stem Systems isn’t about the tech—it’s about what the tech enables. Stronger, more human connections. Commercial? Yes. But always human. Because clients don’t care about specs – they care about outcomes.

  3. 02 Photo

    We didn’t shoot control panels or server racks. We captured what matters: clients connecting with their customers. Because that’s what Stem Systems really delivers.

  4. 03 Colour

    Research found an industry drenched in blue. So we chose red - confident, warm and a little unexpected. Paired with rich burgundy and soft off-whites, the palette builds trust while keeping it human. Familiar, but not generic.

  5. 04 Icon

    The mark captures how a single human connection can grow, evolve and branch into many. A nod to the compounding impact Stem Systems has on its clients, and its forward-thinking ethos.

  6. 05 Typography

    Set in Alliance No.1 – a sans-serif chosen for its clarity and versatility. With clean strokes, subtle inktraps, and open apertures, it stays readable across every touchpoint. Functional, but with just enough personality to feel considered.

  7. Bringing the brand to life

    Bringing the brand to life: As Stem expands into the Republic of Ireland, this new identity is designed to scale, maintaining a sense of clarity, warmth, and purpose. DHD has continued to support the brand rollout with photography, film and web design.
